<h4>Presentation</h4>
    <p>
        One of the main skills I learned was learning how to better communicate within a large cohort group that all works together on the same task. I’ve also learned a lot about metadata and researched a bit of it on my own. The scale of this project is fairly large, so with more communication and knowledge on metadata helped me see the bigger picture of the project. I’m so glad I was able to talk to the museum themselves and understand why they chose to start the collaboration with UCF. Gerg Holbrook, executive director, had told me the NSB city had asked him for information on an officer from the area, but found that all of his documents were basically useless since it is still in the book. He wanted to be able to make it easier for people to find information from The Pelican that will last for many generations to come. From this, I saw that an OCR would just solve exactly what they need to come out of the digitization. I wish I had more time to develop that for them or find a cheaper alternative for the museum (perhaps a summer project). Being a part of this I was really great for interviews as I was able to talk about it for my incoming new graduate job. I’m super thankful that I was able to join in on the project, and again, thank you for reaching out!
